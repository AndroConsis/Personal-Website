Tidy doesn't find anything to clean

Tidy can only look inside your Home folder after you grant access once. Open Tidy, go to Settings → Home folder, click Grant Access… and confirm the preselected Home folder. Then click Rescan.

What do SAFE, REVIEW and MANUAL mean?

  • SAFE — caches and build data that their tool rebuilds on its own. "Clean all safe items" and scheduled auto-clean only ever touch these.
  • REVIEW — probably fine to remove, but it costs something (a re-download, a rebuild, or wiping a simulator's data), so Tidy waits for your click.
  • MANUAL — owned by macOS or another app. Tidy explains the right way to free the space, or shows the item in Finder for you.

How do I get something back?

Files Tidy removes go to the Trash. Open the Trash, select the item and choose File → Put Back. Simulator erases and simulator runtime removals can't be undone from the Trash; reinstall runtimes from Xcode → Settings → Components.

How do I uninstall an app with Tidy?

Open Apps, choose Uninstall… next to the app, and untick any leftover files you want to keep. The first time, Tidy asks you to grant your Applications folder once. Apps installed by the App Store or by an installer belong to macOS, so Tidy tells you up front and shows the app in Finder for you to drag to the Trash; it then offers to remove the app's leftover files.

Why can't Tidy delete some items itself?

Tidy follows the Mac App Store's security rules and never asks for your administrator password. Items owned by macOS, such as leftover system simulator caches or apps from the App Store, are shown in Finder so you can remove them yourself.

Can Tidy empty the Trash?

No. macOS lets only Finder empty the Trash. In Junk Files → Trash, click Open Finder, then press Shift-Command-Delete or choose Finder → Empty Trash. Until you empty it, anything Tidy removed can be put back.

What do Large Files and Disk Map look at?

Both look through your Home folder, but only when you start them, and they read sizes, not the contents of your files. The first time, macOS may ask whether Tidy can access your Desktop or Documents folder. Disk Map also tells you which app or tool a folder belongs to, whether that app is still installed, and whether the folder is safe to remove. Nothing is removed without your approval.

Simulator items aren't showing up

Simulator runtimes, devices and caches are detected through Xcode's command-line tools. Make sure Xcode is installed in /Applications and has been opened at least once.

Does Tidy run in the background?

Tidy lives in your menu bar and re-checks weekly while it's running. To keep it running after a restart, turn on Settings → Open Tidy at login. Automatic cleaning is off unless you turn it on, and it only ever removes SAFE items.
